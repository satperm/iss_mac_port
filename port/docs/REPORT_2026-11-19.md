## Findings

- **The bright second after a save loads is the game's own fog blend.** For ~0.6 s after a load the full-screen fog
  pass (ps f3ce73fa) gets different constants and brightens the HDR scene by ~1.4 log units (captures and pass dumps
  right after a load and 4 s later). It happens with background compilation on or off. The eye adaptation is not it:
  the adapted exposure stays at its clamp (9.85) the whole time in the chase scene. Not a port bug; no fix planned.
- **The particle chain on the GPU is already parallel.** With MoltenVK's concurrent compute encoders off
  (`MVK_TLG_CONCURRENT_DISPATCH=0`) the chain takes 7.7 instead of 1.5 ms on the GPU and the truck drops 83 -> 56
  FPS. `tools/gpu_probes/mq6.mm`: 520 one-workgroup dispatches take 0.13-0.16 ms concurrent and 1.1-3.2 ms serial;
  their order (interleaved shaders or grouped) does not matter. No cheap lever left there.
- **Tessellated rect/quad lists** (truck ~110, village ~215 indirect a frame) do not split render passes noticeably:
  Bloodborne's #39 idea does not apply.
- **GPU-bound (4K output) for weaker Macs:** keeping passes across own-attachment feedback barriers gains ~1% (not
  worth the stale-read risk). Village 4K GPU profile: the fire/smoke particle pass (vs bbd2d927 / ps 24dad4be) is
  6.3 of 39 ms a frame; GPU particles off: village 31.0 -> 35.8 FPS at 4K. Rendering particles at half resolution
  could give weak Macs up to ~10% in fire scenes; multi-day work, owner's decision.
