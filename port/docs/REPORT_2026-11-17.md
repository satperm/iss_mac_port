## Open: auto exposure too dark (owner's report)

Screen goes dark for 2-3 s after a camera change (e.g. falling onto the lower Space Needle platform). Found so far:

- Gone with GPU particles off.
- `TLG_EXPOSURE_LOG=1` (branch agent/exposure-log) hooks the CPU sum of the per-tile luminance
  (eboot+0x171783, 225 log-luminance values). In the owner's dark moments the mean jumps from -0.08 to -0.03:
  the game measures a brighter scene while the picture gets darker, then recovers over 2-3 s. No NaN/Inf.
- So GPU particles add light to the luminance input that the final image does not show.
- The F11 frame capture did not record in split mode: to fix before the next owner session.
