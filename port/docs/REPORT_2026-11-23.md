## Half-resolution particles (#75)

The fire and smoke pass (ps 24dad4be) blends premultiplied colour over the RGBA16F scene. It has no depth
attachment and one colour output. Its draws now go to a half-size image: rgb holds the accumulated colour and a holds
the scene's transmittance. When the pass ends, one full-screen pass composites that image: scene * a + rgb.

| Scene | Off | Half |
|---|---|---|
| Village 4K | 31.1 | 35.2 |
| Village 1080p | 76.9 | 80.1 |
| Truck 4K / 1080p (2 x 60 s) | 37.5 / 83.8 | 39.1 / 85.3 |
| Bridge 4K / 1080p | 50.9 / 110.8 | 50.8 / 111.2 |
| Seattle 4K / 1080p | 42.1 / 81.1 | 42.0 / 82.9 |

It also works with the launcher's render scale: at 67% the half-size image is 643x362. Chase, smoke and coals
ran 120 s each with no crash lines. A 20-minute village soak held 80.8 FPS with memory flat at 1.5 GB. In the 4K
PNG screenshots the fires look the same, with slightly softer edges.

## #71: Rosetta abort with particles Off

Both reports end with this line:

    rosetta error: unexpectedly need to EmulateForward on a synchronous exception x86_rip=0x80062307a

At eboot+0x62307a the game's Gnm command writer stores 32 bytes with `vmovups [rcx+0x20], ymm1`. With
particles Off (`TLG_UNIFIED_MEMORY=0`) the command buffer pages are write-tracked: constants live inside
command buffers. Rosetta wrote the first half of the store, faulted on the second half in the next page, and
aborted. It does not reproduce on macOS 27.2: truck and chase ran 120 s each with Off + MetalFX and about
3,000 write faults/s, without a crash. Draft reply:
`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-leonidsokurov-bloodborne-mac-port/18e40170-004f-4fb6-b3b5-73f994c2bc1b/scratchpad/issue71-reply.md`
(asks the player to use Auto).

## Closed without gain

- **Distributed reader lock for `programs_mutex`:** the draw-preparation workers spend about 29% of their time
  in `TlgRwLock::lock_shared`. Slots per thread were about 1% slower (truck 85.4 -> 84.4, village 77.1 -> 75.7).
- **Draw-preparation worker count:** 7 / 3 / 1 workers gave 85.8 / 80.4 / 83.1; the default stays.
- **Half resolution for other passes:** the other blended RGBA16F passes without depth are full-screen post
  effects (vs b94242a8) or multiplicative. Only 24dad4be qualifies.
- At 1080p the M3 Max is limited by the game's own threads. The main thread spends about half its time in the
  game's rdtsc usleep spin (eboot 0x53b2b0); a real sleep there was already measured on 2026-11-14 (no gain).

## Afternoon

- **#78 merged: auto exposure at a 4K output.** The 4K picture was too dark in general: the burning village at 4K had
  a clearly darker frame than at 1080p, and the coals scene went dark in 2 of 4 runs. At 4K the game sums 900
  luminance tiles, but the GPU measures only about 21 of the 30 tile rows. Tiles 625-899 come back 0, NaN or
  garbage (down to -1.7e38) on every other call. The game's ring of measurements also keeps a running float sum
  of values around 1e-20, which drifted below zero. Now those tiles take the median, and the ring's sum is
  recomputed exactly. 4K coals: 5 of 5 runs steady. Village 4K luma 61 against 63 at 1080p. 1080p is
  unchanged. 5120x1440 (800 tiles) had the same unmeasured tiles; 1440p and 3440x1440 had none.
