## Details

1. **shadPS4 #5753:** split buffer loads whose V# comes from a phi. Our precompile logs 40
   `MarkReadConstBufferSharpSources` asserts (20 failed records) every first start. Port, then count failed
   records, compare screenshots (truck, Seattle, village) and FPS (lib_ab, 3 x 60 s).
2. **`Perf:` line every 5 s:** frame-time p50/p99/p99.9 and held frames, CPU % of each named port thread in
   both processes (`thread_info`), GPU busy % (Metal command buffer GPU start/end, through a MoltenVK
   completion hook if `VK_EXT_metal_objects` can't give the buffers), draws, passes, dispatches, new
   pipelines, protection faults and copied bytes, footprint of both processes, compressor/swap, thermal
   state. Cost check: truck and bridge with and without, 3 x 60 s, within the +-1% noise band.
3. **shadPS4 #5770:** read-only buffers with `num_records = 0xFFFFFFFF` are clamped to ~512 MB and their pages
   protected again at every dispatch (41k faults/s in God of War). Count such bindings and the fault rate in
   ISS; if they matter, port the cap and measure.
4. **Held frames:** log which draws are skipped and why (module reload after the precompile's release, cache
   eviction, something else); fix if cheap.
5. **Matrix script** (`scripts/macos/perf_matrix.sh`): truck and Seattle at 1080p, village and bridge at 4K,
   Seattle traversal, on a given bundle; one JSON line per run to `out/perf/history.jsonl`.
6. **Causal sweep:** `TLG_STAGE_DELAY_*` for stage A, stage B and the recorder on the CPU and GPU scenes;
   table of FPS sensitivity per stage.
7. **Detailed benchmark:** 60 s at the player's settings, 30 s at half render scale, `report.json` and a
   verdict. Align the wording with iss_pc #881 ("Your last session": GPU, processor, frame rate limit).
8. **shadPS4 #5745:** texture cache image freed twice (GC on the GPU thread, unmap on the game thread).
9. **`pad-test`:** the touchpad tap check at `test_pad.c:98` fails on slow VMs; wait on the state, not a time.
10. **Fresh bundle:** the very first start after unzipping failed twice (status 15 during the shader listing,
    "GPU process could not start").
