# Plan for 2026-11-22/23 (after 0.1.16)

Goal unchanged: performance (120 FPS on the owner's M3 Max, 30 FPS on weaker Macs), weaker Macs first.
Every change: lib_ab 3 x 60 s on truck, bridge, Seattle, village (+ coals save `out/coals-save-20261121`),
stability suite, no FPS regressions; picture-changing defaults wait for the owner's eyes.

## Order

1. **Write-fault window 1 MiB on Macs with 24 GB or less** (Bloodborne PR #161). ISS has the same window
   (`buffer_cache.cpp`, 256 KiB). Bloodborne on a 16 GB M3: 20.5 -> 26.6 FPS in heavy scenes, a third fewer faults.
   Measure fault counts here with `hw.memsize` faked by an env override; ask issue #58 (M3 Air 16 GB) to test.
2. **shadPS4 PRs that cut CPU work per draw** (our DrawRec/GPU thread is the limit at 1080p on the M3 Max):
   #5731 descriptor update templates, #5726 skip redundant pipeline binds, #5652 combine pending image
   transitions. Port the ones that apply to our fork, measure each.
3. **Half-resolution fire/smoke pass** (owner approved for weaker Macs; design #65): the pass vs bbd2d927 /
   ps 24dad4be is 6.3 of 39 ms at 4K in the village. Behind `TLG_PARTICLES_HALF=1`; 2-3 days.
4. **Object motion texture per size** (Bloodborne #160: it waited on the GPU when sizes alternated): check
   whether ISS's `vk_object_motion.cpp` waits the same way (only with an upscaler using object motion).
5. **Correctness backports from shadPS4:** #5668 CLEAR_STATE defaults (merged), #5723-5725 recompiler 64-bit
   shifts (merged), #5709 CountingSemaphore timeouts on macOS (open), #5732/#5745 free/unmap images on the GPU
   thread (open, stability).
6. **Softer async hold:** do not hold a frame when only a few small draws were skipped (fewer hitches in new
   places); needs the owner's eyes.
7. **Coals darkness:** two runs on the coals save with `TLG_EXPOSURE_LOG=1`, fix on/off, screenshots every 5 s.
8. **Long sessions:** a 30-minute soak in Seattle (the Castlesea Windows port of ISS had FPS fall to 1-3 after
   8-10 minutes from a full material cache; our renderer is different, but check the trend).
